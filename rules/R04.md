# R-04: medical references in advertising text

CV-04-v1 is the exact second sentence of Article27(2), signed ND15/2018,
PDF page19. It is a narrower extract of the complete paragraph already recorded
as human-confirmed by Dung in CV-02-v1. It retains that human confirmation's
timestamp, source-byte SHA-256 and demo applicability; this extraction does not
claim a new human review. The agent visually compared page19 again. Source and
continuation provenance are in `nd15-review.json`.

R-04 is enabled for the already-confirmed TPBVSK advertising scope. A closed
dictionary in `endorsement.py` covers explicit doctor/pharmacist/medical-provider
recommendations and text referring to medical images, names, attire, equipment,
letters, articles or patient thank-you letters. It creates `needs_review` items,
not an automatic legal conclusion. Actual image content and implicit endorsement
are outside this text-only check. A medical term by itself does not fire.

Safety-advice and explicit prohibition/negation phrases are excluded. The
dictionary is deliberately narrow; complex negation and indirect endorsement
still require human review. Normalized matching never changes source evidence:
findings carry untouched Unicode text and code-point offsets, including NFD and
emoji. `rules_checked` counts R-04 once even for several records or phrases.

Tests call the actual existing validator without modifying `detect/`. The source
metadata reuses the prior human verification rather than fabricating `verified_by`
or a new review time. Pending R-05..R-08 remain disabled. Canonical layer flags are
unchanged. The public generation-reference examples remain development-only.

Acceptance: `python -m pytest rules/tests/test_r04.py rules/tests/test_sources.py
rules/tests/test_engine.py -q`, followed by `make fmt FORMAT_PATHS="rules"`,
`make lint`, `make test`, `make eval` and `make smoke` on Python3.11/Linux.

Linux Python3.11 `make fmt FORMAT_PATHS=rules`, `make lint`, `make test` (277 passed,

Negation follow-up: a product attribute such as `không đường` must not suppress
an affirmative medical recommendation later in the same sentence. Prefix
negation is now limited to the fixed grammatical forms immediately preceding
the medical reference. Two regressions failed before this fix;33 focused tests
and the full348-test Linux suite, lint/eval/current smoke passed afterward.
The source, rule scope and quote-offset behavior are unchanged.
